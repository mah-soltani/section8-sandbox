# Dataset source

`face.csv` is the unchanged 119-race U.S. Senate dataset from Kosuke Imai's *Quantitative Social Science: An Introduction*, Chapter 4 (Prediction).

Source: https://github.com/kosukeimai/qss/blob/master/PREDICTION/face.csv

Downloaded November 15, 2026 (Pacific time). Variables: `year`, `state`, `winner`, `loser`, `w.party`, `l.party`, `d.comp`, `r.comp`, `d.votes`, `r.votes`.

Competence shares measure perceived competence from photographs, rather than actual competence. Vote shares in this exercise are two-party shares.
